# Original demo assets

Paper Lanterns, Small Signals and Open Window are original project demonstration verses with procedural musical backing. Their audio uses synthetic speech generated by the system's Flite voice through ffmpeg. These are spoken-word fixtures, not sung performances or recordings of named commercial artists.

The original verses, procedural backing, catalog metadata and generated demo recordings may be used, modified and shared with this project. No third-party song lyrics or music recordings were downloaded or copied. Flite/ffmpeg software and voices retain their own licenses; no engine binaries or voice models are redistributed in this folder. No human performer, Spotify artist or endorsement is implied by the label Muse Demo Ensemble.

Each WAV is 36 seconds, mono PCM16 at 16 kHz. LRC cue times indicate when each synthetic spoken line starts; the final empty cue at 34 seconds clears the words. The local recognizer supports the exact supplied recordings, not other music with a similar name.

Rebuild with `python tools/build_demo_catalog.py` on a development computer that has ffmpeg with the flite source filter. Do not run the generator on the glasses. The app itself does not output these recordings; use tools/demo_player.html or a normal external player for acoustic testing.
